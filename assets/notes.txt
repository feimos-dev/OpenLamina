Controller models

The controllers' models in assets/controllers are the WebXR Input Profiles' assets
(https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles, packages/assets, version 1.0.20 on npm),
Copyright (c) 2019 Amazon, under the MIT licence (assets/controllers/LICENSE.md, shipped as
LICENSE_webxr-input-profiles.txt). They are as published, but for the Valve Index ones, whose textures are
downscaled to 1024 pixels. scripts/rendermodels.py converts them to SteamVR's render-model layout at build time.
